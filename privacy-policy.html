Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 November 2026.

Flowrule does not collect any data.

There is no account to create, no sign up, and no server for the app to talk to. What you enter, your calculations, settings and saved projects, is stored only on your device.

What leaves your device

Nothing, unless you choose to share it. The app makes no network requests of its own. It contains no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting service and no third party software development kits of any kind. If you export a PDF, CSV or project file, it goes only where you send it with the share sheet.

What I can see

Nothing. There is no way for me to read what you enter, because it never reaches me.

Siri and Shortcuts

The Shortcuts actions run on your device inside the app and use the same calculations. Apple's handling of Siri requests is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

Deleting a project removes it. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Purchases

The app is free. The Pro unlock is a single purchase handled entirely by the App Store. Apple processes it and I never see your payment details. The app asks Apple whether the unlock is owned and stores nothing but the answer. Apple's own privacy policy covers that transaction.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects nothing, from anyone, at any age.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
